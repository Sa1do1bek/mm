## 2. Approaches and recommendation

1. **Recommended: extend current cost ledger and invoice header with line evidence and command services.** Keep established stock costing, exact transfer lineage, approval framework, invoice finance linkage and close cockpit. Add bounded matching, discrepancy, acquisition-adjustment and receipt-clearing services. A transactional bridge writes stock/cost/clearing evidence together. This preserves working features and supports a small warehouse-first slice, but requires shared contracts before implementation.
2. **Enrich existing amount-based forms and add validation flags.** Smallest first change, but cannot prove per-line partial matching, overbilling prevention, consumed-cost corrections or historical closure. Reject as completion strategy; an enriched summary can remain a compatibility projection.
3. **Separate invoice verification/valuation service with event-only integration.** Independent deployment and strong boundary are possible, but distributed consistency and migration duplicate the ledger and receipt evidence already present. Reserve this for an explicit architecture decision; unnecessary for the requested completion.

## 3. Proposed defaults for the single user review gate

These are proposed business choices, not assertions about Uzbek accounting or tax law. The coordinator should present them together; implementation must use approved policy versions. Suggested settings below are review candidates for explicitly configured new policies, never replacements for existing approved accounting, tolerance, period or non-moving-stock policies. Missing required configuration blocks the relevant operation rather than silently applying a suggestion.

| Decision | Proposed default |
|---|---|
| Carrying-cost policy | Use the approved company/material policy version, optionally warehouse-specific only when explicitly configured. There is no universal new carrying-cost default: finance explicitly selects and identifies the behavior. Preserve existing policy/history until finance maps it. ACTUAL_COST legacy behavior must be explicitly identified as the existing weighted algorithm or migrated with approval; unmapped legacy policy remains visible and blocks migration rather than being silently reinterpreted. Support FIFO and SPECIFIC_BATCH as carrying-cost alternatives. Preserve approved LIFO compatibility; analytical market/NRV/lower-of-cost methods remain available and require a separately approved adjustment to change carrying value. |
| Weighted versus moving average | For explicitly selected new policy versions, moving average recalculates after each eligible receipt/value event; periodic weighted average uses opening value plus period receipts, values issues provisionally, then posts a visible period adjustment. These descriptions do not relabel existing approved policies or change the current algorithm without approval. SPECIFIC_BATCH consumes the identified batch/layer. Policy change applies prospectively at a reconciled period boundary; no rewriting prior issues. |
| Inventory basis | No negative quantity/value and no unexplained zero-cost owned receipt. Explicit approved free-of-charge/recovered-material valuation is permitted through a reasoned valuation document, not a bypass. Owned stock at external holders stays valued; foreign-owner custody/consignment stays outside company inventory value until ownership transfer. Status or holder changes alone do not recognize an expense. |
| Quantity/price tolerance | Default zero business tolerance. Exact decimal UOM conversion; no quantity over accepted unreversed/unallocated evidence. Only deterministic currency rounding allowance of one currency minor unit per invoice, separately recorded, is automatic. Configurable nonzero price tolerance needs finance approval and a versioned policy; never allows absent quantity, wrong supplier/owner/currency or duplicate identity. |
| Invoice identity | Require supplier number/date on registration. Unique `(tenant, company, supplier, documentKind, supplierFiscalYear, normalizedNumber)`. Normalize Unicode NFKC, outer whitespace and case; keep punctuation/internal characters to avoid merging distinct numbers. Same financial year by default equals invoice-date calendar year, configurable before use. Distinct companies may reuse a number. Issued/cancelled identities remain reserved; replacement revises the registered document or links a correction, never evades uniqueness via deletion. |
| Drafts and partial invoices | Allow multiple draft invoices per PO. Drafts do not reserve accepted quantity. Submission atomically reserves exact evidence; blocked submitted invoices keep their valid reservations until withdrawn/cancelled, visibly identified for resolution. Excess or ineligible quantity is never reserved. All lines must be resolved before final issue; operator may create separate invoices for the valid subset. |
| Tax/currency | Store net, tax and gross separately. Recoverable tax is excluded from acquisition cost; approved nonrecoverable tax/duties may be capitalized. Finance supplies tax classification and rate/FX source, date and version. No hardcoded tax rate or FX=1 for foreign currency. Invoice currency must match the PO revision unless an approved amendment supplies the conversion contract. FX variance is separately handed to finance, not buried as material price variance. |
| Acquisition expenses | Capitalize approved transport, customs and directly attributable agreed expenses against explicit receipt scope. VALUE is the default basis using original net acquisition base value; QUANTITY only for comparable base UOM, WEIGHT requires recorded weights, MANUAL requires allocations summing exactly to the document. Missing basis is an error, never equal-share fallback. |

Negative correction cannot make carrying value negative; route excess to a reviewed variance decision. |
| Receipt clearing | Clear at receipt expected net base value for the matched quantity. Invoice-price/tax/FX variance is recorded separately. Unbilled accepted receipts are normal accrual/open items, not automatically overdue invoices and not automatically close blockers. Reconcile them to FI and retain them across periods. |
| Close/reopen | Monthly company close by default; warehouse subclose supported with hierarchical overlap locks. Require all material discrepancies resolved or explicitly approved as carry-forward accruals with owner, amount and due date. ## 4. Data and domain contracts

### 4.1 Valuation and acquisition cost (MM-09-01, MM-09-02, MM-09-03)

Extend the existing `InventoryCostLayer`, `StockCostMovement` and posting command with the section 05 exact stock position and canonical immutable `StockContext` reference, plus the section 01 UOM snapshot. Required fields: tenant/ledger company, material, location hierarchy, batch/serial lineage and context ID resolving legal ownerPartyId, typed holder, custody agreement and typed earmark; approved valuation area; source document + source line + posting ID; document date; **postingDate**; actual recorded timestamp; base quantity/UOM; original currency/net value; base currency/value; FX snapshot ID; policy version; and reversal/correction source. Responsibility/MOL assignments are separate evidence, not an alternate ownership or physical-holder key. Warehouse/holder/location describe where stock is held; legal ownership determines whose value it is. The approved policy defines valuation pooling, while exact source context remains traceable; internal bin/status and custody changes carry value without automatic repricing. External holder areas follow Section 06's location contract. A layer may carry an external location instead of inventing a physical internal warehouse; schema/API nullability is coordinated with section 05. Operational state and VMI management remain independent of StockContext ownership semantics.

Ledger deltas distinguish physical quantity change from a cost adjustment's **affectedQuantity**. Value-only corrections have quantityDelta=0; existing landed-cost rows currently record affected quantity in quantity. An additive movement-type-aware projection must map historical entries correctly rather than summing every legacy quantity. Section 05's posting authority applies stock-balance projection changes; Section 09 returns cost/layer effects through that shared command boundary. Each authorized layer/balance update and ledger posting occurs in one transaction under the same stock key locks; Section 09 services do not directly write the stock-balance projection. The sum of base-currency allocation deltas equals the source delta exactly; rounding remainder goes to the last stable allocation ID and is visible. Internal amount scale 4 and unit-price scale 6 preserve current storage compatibility; approved rounding policy applies consistently and currency minor-unit rounding happens at document/export boundaries. Monetary APIs use decimal strings.

Add `InventoryCostAdjustment` and `InventoryCostAdjustmentLine`: kind PRICE/ACQUISITION_EXPENSE/VARIANCE/REVERSAL, reason, approved source invoice/expense, affected receipt lines, business date, currency/rate, signed amount, original adjustment ID, version, and split destinations (on hand, owned custody, transit, consumed/disposed cost object). Use DRAFT → SUBMITTED → APPROVED → POSTED, with reject/withdraw before post and linked reversal after post. A price-only credit has zero physical quantity delta and does not restore invoiceable quantity. A quantity credit restores only the original allocated quantity; a physical supplier return remains a separate section 08 posting. Cost reductions plus already-issued credits are bounded by original and prior corrections.

`LandedCostAllocation` remains the allocation document/UI vocabulary; generalize its source to receipt collection with optional ImportDelivery and approved expense components. Persist source-component allocation IDs with unique prevention of over-allocation, original basis, document/base amounts and targeted receipt/layer descendants. Post via the cost adjustment engine, not a separate direct balance mutation. Posted allocations cannot be edited; revision creates signed adjustment evidence. Existing valuation/impairment records stay supported, with an explicit visible distinction between analytical result and carrying-value posting.

### 4.2 Invoice matching and discrepancy lifecycle (MM-09-04, MM-09-05)

Keep `ContractInvoice` identity for existing finance consumers, add kind INVOICE/CREDIT_NOTE/DEBIT_NOTE, originalInvoiceId, normalized identity, verificationStatus and versioned totals. Keep paidAmount/settlement status finance-owned. New verification state: DRAFT → SUBMITTED → BLOCKED or MATCHED → APPROVED → ISSUED. Clarification adds a revision, reopens matching and invalidates earlier approval; user cannot mark a discrepancy resolved by a free-form status edit. Existing `/issue` delegates to this state machine and cannot bypass it.

Add:

- `ContractInvoiceLine`: ordinal, PO + immutable PO revision/line ID, type MATERIAL/SERVICE/WORK/ASSET/ACQUISITION_EXPENSE, description, invoice/base UOM and conversion snapshot, quantity, unit net price, net/tax/gross, currency and tax classification. Start with one PO per invoice to preserve the header contract; multiple receipts/acts per line are supported. A supplier invoice spanning POs is represented by one registered supplier invoice envelope with PO-specific children and one identity/totals control, not multiple independent duplicate numbers. This envelope is required before enabling multi-PO entry, and stays in scope.
- `InvoiceReceiptAllocation`: invoice line, typed acceptance source + line ID, accepted base quantity or milestone fraction, expected receipt value, allocated quantity/value, reservation/clearing state, source version and original/reversal allocation ID. An allocation never uses a header total as proof for a specific line. Service/work milestones carry an agreed fraction/amount basis from section 03; materials use base quantity.
- `InvoiceMatchRun` / `InvoiceMatchResult`: immutable input versions, accepted/reversed/reserved/previously invoiced quantities, order and invoiced prices/net/tax/FX, policy version, calculated deltas and decision codes. Revalidation at approval and issue rejects stale acceptance/PO/tax/tolerance inputs.
- `InvoiceDiscrepancy`: QUANTITY, PRICE, TAX, CURRENCY, UOM, MISSING_ACCEPTANCE, QUALITY_HOLD, DUPLICATE, SOURCE_REVERSED; expected/actual/delta, owner, attachments, resolution and approver. Clarification, approved PO amendment, missing acceptance and supplier correction are explicit resolutions. Tolerance release may resolve price variance only within configured permission/limits. Supplier correspondence is prepared/stored in MM; sending requires the existing authorized communication workflow, never automatic side effects of matching.

The matcher processes every line independently, then totals the invoice; opposite line variances cannot cancel each other. New immutable receipt price snapshots replace the current multiplication by live PO price. Acceptance eligibility is source-specific and current at issue; quality release cannot change historical accepted amount silently. A return/reversal after issue generates a correction-required case and reverses only its receipt/clearing/cost effects. It never erases an issued invoice or a finance payment. Existing service/asset payment entry points must use the same verification readiness contract to prevent bypass.

A supplier credit received before original invoice approval may reference the registered blocked original. Match the original and its linked correction as one versioned verification package: approval and issue are atomic for the package, matched physical quantity is counted only once, and payable handoff contains both supplier documents with their net total. For an already-issued original, the correction is approved and sent independently with its original finance reference. Corrections cannot use the netting rule to hide mismatches on unrelated lines. For multi-PO invoices, the envelope owns the unique supplier identity; children use internal IDs and share that envelope, rather than competing for the supplier-number constraint. A submitted line requesting more than available evidence reserves none of that line; other fully valid lines may remain reserved. Users explicitly split a line/invoice when they want to proceed with its available subset.

### 4.3 Receipt clearing and period evidence (MM-09-06, MM-09-07)

Add append-only `ReceiptClearingEntry` keyed by owner/company, supplier, PO line, acceptance line, posting date, currency, base quantity and base net expected value. ## 6. Permissions, audit, partials, reversals and concurrency

- Preserve current ACCOUNTANT draft and FINANCE_CONTROLLER issue distinction. Add scoped capabilities `INVOICE_MATCH`, `INVOICE_VERIFY_APPROVE`, `INVOICE_VARIANCE_APPROVE`, `COST_ADJUSTMENT_POST`, `RECEIPT_RECONCILE`, `PERIOD_CLOSE`, `PERIOD_REOPEN`; map them centrally with section 10. Accountant prepares/matches; finance controller approves variance/cost/issue; warehouse/procurement resolve only their receipt/order evidence; auditor reads/export history. Creator cannot be final approver, including admin acting in the business flow; emergency access needs separate audited procedure, not a blanket bypass.
- Every linked PO, receipt, act, component, correction, warehouse and reconciliation must share tenant/company and authorized data scope. A broad company-read privilege is not authority to mutate another company's records. Intercompany transfer produces two authorized company ledgers; no unilateral cross-company cost edit.
- All successful state transitions record actor/time, business date, reason, before/after version, attachment IDs, policy version, source versions, correlation and idempotency IDs. Failed validation and denied actions use section 10 security/command audit. Posted monetary evidence is immutable, including after source document cancellation.
- Partial receipt/invoice/return/correction quantities accumulate against exact source allocations. Submitted valid reservations plus issued allocations cannot exceed unreversed eligible acceptance. Price-only credits change value only; quantity credits may release allocation only once and in conjunction with actual receipt/return facts. A receipt reversal reduces eligibility and creates a resolution case for already-issued invoices; it does not make negative residual disappear.
- Reversal commands lock original and remaining reversible allocations; partial reversal <= original minus previous reversals. Reverse stock/layer/clearing/variance evidence together or reject atomically. Reversing an old cost adjustment after consumption follows lineage to current stock and consumed variance; it must not blindly decrement the original layer. Ledger-only reverse endpoints must be internal or delegate to a complete domain command.
- Lock order: company-period fence → sorted evidence/PO line IDs → document/identity → sorted stock key/layer IDs → clearing entries. Use a company-level period lock row acquired for every stock/value/clearing mutation and close/reopen in the warehouse-first implementation; this conservative serialization also protects empty/new periods and warehouse/company overlap. It can later be refined with evidence that concurrency and close invariants remain intact. A plain `existsClosed` check alone is insufficient.
- Unique constraints cover supplier-invoice identity, source event/posting + allocation sequence, invoice/receipt allocation revisions, cost-component allocations and outbox event IDs. Aggregate quantity/amount caps are rechecked inside source-row locks; a uniqueness constraint alone cannot enforce a sum. Version checks handle stale forms but do not replace these locks. Locks and state changes remain in one database transaction; outbox dispatch follows commit.
