## 3. Approaches and recommendation

1. **Recommended: extend existing posting and transfer contracts.** Retain StockBalance as a projection, movement allocations/cost lineages as posting evidence, and shipment/transit facts as transfer evidence. Add StockContext identity, state balances and immutable state events; build relocation, one-step, workshop and intercompany commands on those foundations. This preserves working UI and costing and supports a warehouse-first vertical delivery. All existing writers must migrate together at the stock contract cutover.
2. **Minimal columns and more generic forms.** Add blocked quantity and extra company/department fields, then reuse generic movement CRUD. Lower initial effort, but owner segregation, state history, receiving authority and paired-company financial behavior remain weak. This does not satisfy the complete requirements.
3. **Replace with a separate event-sourced inventory/WMS service.** Can provide clean separation, but duplicates proven allocation/cost/approval behavior and requires a risky historical migration and distributed consistency rollout. Not justified for this milestone.

The recommendation is incremental, not a ledger replacement. Extract narrowly scoped command services where needed; do not enlarge the already large StockMovementService with every new business workflow.

## 4. Canonical stock contract

### 4.1 Identity and quantities

Per CONTRACT-DECISIONS.md D01, section 6 owns the canonical immutable `StockContext`: `ownerPartyId`, a typed holder reference (`COMPANY`, `EXTERNAL_PARTY`, `EMPLOYEE` or `DEPARTMENT` with scoped ID), optional `custodyAgreementId` and optional typed `earmarkId`. `StockParty` identifies a legal owner; an employee account is never a substitute for legal ownership. Responsible employee/MOL assignment is separate from physical holder: changing warehouse responsibility does not create a physical movement. VMI responsibility remains independent metadata, outside the stock key. Section 5 incorporates `stockContextId` into exact balance keys, source selection, reservation allocations, posting evidence, batch/cost selectors and transit snapshots. No second identity model is introduced. Section 5 is the sole quantity posting authority: section 7 accountability events are atomic evidence/projections of those postings, and section 9 owns their associated cost effects. Other services call the shared command rather than directly writing balance projections.

The existing key becomes `(tenant, ledgerCompany, material, warehouse, location, bin, batch, stockContextId)`. Serial identities continue through MaterialUnit allocation. Location master data from section 1 identifies normal bins and tanks; a tank uses the location identity and does not require a competing `tankId` dimension. Source/destination warehouse/company/location/bin consistency is checked server-side.

Add child state projections `StockStateBalance(balanceId, stateCode, quantity, version)` with unique balance/state and immutable `StockStateEvent` facts containing before/after state, quantity, document/line/original-event IDs, timestamp, posting date, actor, reason, UOM snapshot and operation ID. Initial states: `USABLE`, `QUARANTINE`, `BLOCKED`, `REJECTED`. Additional configured states require an explicit usability/disposition policy; unknown states are never issuable. Reservations allocate only USABLE quantities.

Compatibility columns on StockBalance remain transactional projections during migration, never a second write authority:

- `physical = sum(state quantity)` at a physical stock position.
- `reserved = active reservations allocated to USABLE stock`; `free = USABLE − reserved`.
- `physical = free + reserved + quarantine + blocked + rejected + other restricted states`.
- Each quantity is nonnegative; `reserved <= USABLE`. Prohibit negative stock for these warehouse workflows even if a broader valuation setting allows it.
- `transit = sum(immutable transit deltas)`; it is separate from physical stock at either warehouse. Incoming/outgoing columns are views of the same allocation, not two assets. Group totals count it once by owner. Incoming transit is not free stock.
- Stock location and stock state are independent. Moving quarantined material preserves quarantine. Ownership change is a separate authorized event, never a side effect of selecting a warehouse.

A stock-state command writes immutable state/allocation evidence and updates projections atomically, with zero change in physical quantity or total value. New operations carry explicit `operationKind` (`RELOCATION`, `STATE_CHANGE`, `TRANSFER_DISPATCH`, etc.); do not overload a zone name or generic status string as inventory meaning.

For unchanged legal ownership, movement between warehouses, transit, employee/workshop custody and external custody preserves the owner's quantity and carried value. Warehouse issue into ordinary-material custody transfers value to the custody position; it does not expense the issued quantity. Only a separately confirmed consumption, approved loss/disposal or actual ownership transfer reduces owned inventory. Owned totals include all owned physical custody and transit once; third-party-owned stock on our premises remains quantitatively visible but excluded from our owned valuation. Section 7 owns consumption evidence and section 9 owns cost effects; section 5 must preserve their lineage across transfers and returns.

Section 1 owns document/base UOM conversion snapshots, material classification and decimal policy. All comparisons/posting use base quantity; display original document quantity and conversion snapshot. Until the approved shared UOM contract is delivered, do not silently convert mass to volume or invent conversion factors. Semifinished goods remain inventory materials classified under the agreed TMB/material-category model, not fixed assets.

### 4.2 Blocking and reservations

Normal reservation remains independent of stock state. A full-batch quality hold must be able to stop reserved goods: atomically split/release affected reservation allocations, write section 2 reservation events and notify affected demand owners, then move quantities to the restricted state. Do not leave a reservation falsely executable on blocked goods. Partial holds choose exact allocations/serial units; default release ordering is latest reservation allocation first, deterministic by creation time and ID, shown in a preview. A quality release restores free stock; it does not silently recreate released reservations. Section 2 can explicitly reserve again according to priority.

Restriction placement is permitted immediately to prevent use, with reason and actor; release requires current quality/authorized disposition evidence. A count freeze prevents ledger-affecting state changes; an urgent safety hold can set a separate non-posting eligibility lock immediately, then be posted through the section 8 freeze/review workflow. This avoids either allowing unsafe issue or rewriting a frozen count baseline.

## 5. Workflows, data and APIs

All following API names are proposed additions/extensions. Reuse existing `/api/transfers` and `/api/stock` conventions. Mutable commands carry `expectedVersion`, actual operation/posting date, reason when exceptional, and `Idempotency-Key`; decimal JSON preserves exact values. Return document version, allowed actions/blockers, operation ID and immutable posting references. Validation failures expose actionable field/line codes; stale state is a conflict with no partial mutation.

### 5.1 Relocation and one-step transfer

Add transfer `purpose = WAREHOUSE | WORKSHOP`, `executionMode = ONE_STEP | TWO_STEP`, and immutable per-operation source/destination snapshots. A same-warehouse document is a relocation; different warehouses are transfers. Retain separate type/endpoint semantics if that yields clearer permissions, but route both through the same stock mutation contract.

`POST /api/stock/relocations` creates an exact-position draft; `/submit`, `/approve`, `/post`, `/reversal-preview`, `/reverse` provide document controls. Quantities can span batches/positions through separate allocations. Post atomically debits/credits exact keys, preserving state, StockContext, cost lineage, serials and expiry. Normal relocation moves free stock. Moving reserved stock requires an explicit reservation-preserving relocation and section 2 allocation reassignment in the same transaction; never consume another order's reservation.

Tanks require compatible material, declared capacity/UOM, mixed-material policy and measured quantity evidence. Default: no mixing different materials or unapproved blending of batches; moving stock preserves original batch identities. Temperature/density-dependent conversions require an approved conversion snapshot. WMS/SCADA measurement may supply evidence but does not independently mutate stock. Concurrency locks the destination capacity resource across materials so two simultaneous fills cannot both pass the same spare-capacity check.

`POST /api/transfers/{id}/post-one-step` creates dispatch and corresponding acceptance facts atomically, immediately clearing transit within the transaction. Use existing exact allocation/cost code, not unrelated generic receipt rows. The actor must have both endpoint permissions; independent document approval remains required. Workflow is available only for same-company warehouse transfers where immediate acceptance is authorized. A failure at destination rolls back the entire operation. UI explains that destination receipt is confirmed now.

Existing `/dispatch` and `/receive` remain for TWO_STEP, multiple shipments and partial allocation receipts. Preserve equations `planned = dispatched + cancelled + stillToDispatch` and `dispatched = acceptedPhysical + returnedToSource + postedLoss + unresolvedTransit`, net of explicit compensation. Damaged physical receipt is part of acceptedPhysical for same-company transport, but remains quarantine and is not usable. Cancel remainder touches only undispatched quantity; closed status requires zero remaining plan, transit and unresolved exceptions. No editing of posted source quantities.

### 5.2 Workshop transfer

Extend documents with source/destination department, stockpoint and responsible recipient plus purpose/production-order references. Section 1 supplies workshop stores and responsible assignments. Section 7 supplies ordinary-material custody positions for stock held outside stores. A workshop-store move is a stock transfer; a change between personal custody positions is section 7's handover using section 5's posting contract. Both link to the same acceptance evidence, not duplicate stock deductions.

Workshop purpose defaults to TWO_STEP and mandatory destination confirmation by an authorized recipient distinct from dispatcher. `POST /api/transfers/{id}/confirm-recipient` accepts exact quantities/serials/condition, posts destination receipt and returns a handover act reference; it shares receipt idempotency and allocation accounting. Partial acceptance is allowed; refusal follows exception flow. An incoming production event cannot impersonate the human recipient without a configured authenticated authorization contract. Transfer does not mean consumption or production completion; those remain section 7 processes. An explicitly approved immediate-consumption command may compose transfer/acceptance and consumption atomically while retaining both facts and their separate cost effects. Semifinished batch genealogy follows the transfer unchanged.

### 5.3 Intercompany transfer

Introduce `IntercompanyTransferAgreement` for permitted company pair, material mappings, UOM mappings, commercial reference, title-transfer rule, currency/price/tax snapshots and roles. Introduce a correlation record `IntercompanyTransfer` and two local documents: sending dispatch in A, receiving document in B. Neither side can edit or approve the other side's local document. Restricted partner summaries expose only agreed transfer facts, not arbitrary cross-company stock or financial data.

Proposed routes: `/api/intercompany-transfers` to create/search; `/{id}/submit-offer`, `/{id}/accept-offer`, `/{id}/dispatch`, `/{id}/receive`, `/{id}/exceptions`, `/{id}/reversal-preview`, `/{id}/reverse`. Company-specific local document IDs are immutable links. Recipient approval validates B's mapping and destination before dispatch. Required MM-05-05 acceptance covers authorized group companies in the existing tenant/application: acceptance posts both companies' linked evidence in one database transaction through an explicitly scoped domain command, not by temporarily granting the caller broad CompanyContext access. Every step revalidates both persisted authorizations and document versions. Cross-tenant/cross-system federation is a conditional adapter only if the approved deployment requires it; it is not a prerequisite for the original intercompany requirement. If activated by that deployment decision, the adapter uses section 10 authenticated outbox/inbox and needs counterpart confirmation/reconciliation before claiming that external exchange works. The required finance/integration handoff applies regardless of whether a federation adapter is needed.

The proposed agreement policy passes title on recipient's accepted quantity; finance must explicitly approve and version it before posting, and it is not a fallback for missing ownership terms. Dispatch: A on-hand decreases, A-owned transit increases; B expected receipt increases, no B owned stock yet. Acceptance: A transit and carrying value clear for that quantity, B stock and acquisition valuation post using section 9's agreed commercial basis. Section 9 receives a paired financial event containing both documents/lines, quantities, A carrying cost, B acquisition basis, price/currency/tax snapshots and reversal references. Financial settlement, payment and statutory posting stay in finance. A failed financial delivery leaves operational acceptance posted with visible pending/error reconciliation, never a silently completed financial state.

Refused stock stays A-owned. If it remains with the carrier it stays transit; if physically unloaded at B pending decision it moves into A-owned/B-held restricted custody under section 6, clearing the corresponding transit quantity without becoming B-owned. Both quantity and A's exact carried value move from transit to custody; neither is lost or expensed by that custody change. Later acceptance transfers title once; return shipment moves that custody into A-owned return transit with the same carried value. Quantity/value reports distinguish legal ownership, custody and intercompany commercial amounts. A transfer-price difference is not hidden as a same-company cost discrepancy. Legal/tax-specific policies require finance configuration; this design does not invent regulatory rules.

### 5.4 Exceptions and controlled state changes

Extend existing TransferException with `REFUSED` and explicit `custodyDisposition`, refused quantity, refusal evidence, responsible party and source allocation link. `POST /api/transfers/{id}/refuse` records refusal without decrementing stock or transit by itself. Required disposition: `WITH_CARRIER`, `HELD_AT_DESTINATION` or `RETURN_DISPATCHED`, with physical confirmation commands controlling the actual ledger changes. For same-company stock physically unloaded, receipt to quarantine/blocked state is recorded even if operational acceptance is refused; the refusal case remains open. For intercompany stock, use foreign-owner custody described above.

Existing missing/loss and damaged-return cases remain. Overlapping unresolved cases may not claim the same units twice. A case can be split into identified partial resolutions with stable original and remaining quantities; review/approval binds a quantity/value/evidence revision. Subsequent change invalidates approval. Receipt of previously reported missing goods reduces the case and clears transit, not a second stock receipt. Returning after receipt creates a reverse-direction shipment and new transit; return does not erase the first receipt. Quarantined damaged returns arrive restricted at source unless new release evidence exists.

`POST /api/stock/state-changes` creates a quantity-level document from exact positions, requested state, reason and source decision; `/preview`, `/submit`, `/approve`, `/post`, `/reverse` follow section 10 controls. Immediate safety restrictions use authorized post policy; usable release always requires section 8 disposition. `REJECTED` stock is physically present but not issuable; disposal still needs a section 8 write-off. Moving between restricted states does not change quantity/value. QualityInspectionService and transfer quality disposition call this common service rather than editing balance fields.

### 5.5 Reversal, correction and WMS boundary

Add transfer-level `GET /api/transfers/{id}/reversal-preview` and `POST /api/transfers/{id}/reverse` with selected original operation/allocation quantities, correction reason and date. Preview lists dependent receipts, reservations, custody transfers, losses, period/freeze blockers, both company documents and financial effects. Posted facts never change. Compensation facts refer to the original allocation/clearance/state event and invert its actual quantities and cost lineage, not today's costing policy. Reversal quantity cannot exceed original minus prior compensations.

An unreceived dispatch can be reversed only if it was a recording error and the exact goods remain physically at source; goods actually coming back require a return. A receipt correction restoring transit is allowed only when goods did not physically arrive or the receipt was overstated, with no downstream allocation. Once accepted stock has been consumed, reserved, relocated or transferred onwards, reverse dependencies through their own authorized documents or use an approved current-period adjustment. Do not silently steal replacement stock from another batch. Corrections in a closed period are posted into an open period with original reference and finance approval; no backdating into a closed period. Add explicit positive transit compensation kinds—the current transit service only permits initial dispatch positive and clearance negative—without rewriting historical entries.

MM remains authoritative for stock/ownership/value documents. WMS owns execution tasks, scans and movement observations within the agreed authority. Replace `InboundIntegrationSyncService.stockBalance` overwrite with reconciliation snapshots/differences; snapshot discrepancy produces a review/count/adjustment task, not an unexplained stock balance edit. Add `POST /api/integrations/wms/stock-operations` with authenticated provider identity, external operation/task ID, MM document/line/version, exact source/destination/StockContext/batch/serial, actual quantity/UOM snapshot, event timestamp and evidence. Validate against the authorized MM task and invoke the same business command; unique provider-operation key makes replay safe. Out-of-order, unknown-map, excessive quantity, frozen/closed-period and revoked-task events enter a visible exception queue. Section 10 owns transport/authentication/replay monitoring; section 5 owns stock command semantics. Existing embedded scanning/putaway and material baseline publishing stay compatible; device routing and laboratory work stay with their existing owners.

## 7. Concurrency, migration and cross-section contracts

Posting transaction includes stock/state/reservation/serial mutations, shipment/transit/cost evidence, audit and outbox. Lock the business document, affected allocation/case, canonical capacity/stock resources and balance/state rows in a documented deterministic order shared by receiving, issue, quality and count code. New absent-key creation remains protected by material/context creation locks plus database uniqueness. Cross-company commands order company IDs then resource keys; material/serial mapping is fixed before locks. A retry with the same key and same payload returns the same authorized outcome; same key with a different payload conflicts. Stock/context identities cannot be changed on populated balances.

Use additive migrations only. Preserve V1 checksum. Coordinator assigns actual Flyway versions and the single writer for shared ledger/migration files. Migration introduces StockContext/state structures and immutable facts, backfills legacy own-company/own-holder contexts only when provenance supports that assumption, and records migration provenance. Existing quarantine remains quarantine; ambiguous foreign ownership, invalid sums, orphan reservations or duplicated keys become reconciliation blockers, never silently merged. - MM is authoritative for stock and valuation; WMS supplies execution evidence and reconciliations.
