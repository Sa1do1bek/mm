## 1. Recommendation and alternatives

**A — recommended: extend existing procurement commands with immutable commercial revisions and quantity/amount evidence.** Keep supplier governance, procurement-method decisions, RFQ/tender publication and evaluation, source allocation, approval adapters, portal collaboration and service/work acceptance. Add the missing request transformation, contract execution, sent-order revision and claim-resolution behavior. Let warehouse, quality and valuation owners perform their postings. Benefits: preserves substantial existing functionality and allows a small procurement dependency slice in the warehouse milestone. Cost: shared contracts and compatibility migrations must be coordinated.

**B — wrap current screens and add only validation/reporting.** Faster initial delivery, but request merge/split, post-send amendments, contract specification execution and evidenced claim closure remain impossible. Insufficient for the full requirement baseline.

**C — replace procurement with a new generic document engine.** Could unify state handling, but duplicates working tender/KYC, supplier portal and approval behavior and requires broad migration. Risk and scope exceed the demonstrated gaps. Reject for this program.

The design uses small new command services around existing entities, not another procurement subsystem. Existing `PurchaseOrderService` already combines many concerns; add revision and obligation services beside it rather than expanding it with all new business logic.

## 3. Proposed business model and commands

### 3.1 Shared invariants and data shapes

Each new persisted business record inherits tenant/company, version, created/updated actor/time and immutable business number. Retain registration time separately from effective operation date. Use `BigDecimal` throughout. The section 01 UOM snapshot supplies document UOM/quantity, base UOM/quantity, conversion ID/version/factor and rounding. Never infer equivalence from equal text labels or recompute historic quantity with a changed factor.

Use stable line IDs through amendments. Each binding contains `purchaseRequestItemId`, `purchaseOrderItemId`, `contractSpecificationId` and the commercial revision IDs that actually authorized it. Optional origins are explicit rather than fabricated. Contract, schedule, demand, receipt, service and claim links must agree on tenant, company, supplier, material/service identity and quantity basis; authorizing access to each ID separately is not sufficient.

For an effective PO line, define:

- `authorized = original authorized quantity + approved increases − approved reductions`.
- `netAccepted = posted accepted receipts/acts − linked acceptance reversals − returns that reverse fulfillment`.
- `openToFulfill = authorized − netAccepted − approvedShortClosed` (never negative).
- A physical receipt or quarantine status alone is not evidence of commercially accepted fulfillment. Commercial acceptance, quality release, ownership and invoice eligibility are separate facts under CONTRACT-DECISIONS.md D04. When the approved commercial policy permits acceptance before quality release, accepted-but-quarantined stock can fulfill the obligation while remaining unavailable for use. Rejected, unaccepted quantities remain open supplier obligations.
- A replacement for a rejected unaccepted unit fills the original outstanding obligation once. A replacement for an already accepted unit requires a linked fulfillment reversal/return before it reopens that obligation. Physical arrival history is never subtracted or overwritten.

Add focused entities, subject to shared-contract naming reconciliation: `PurchaseRequestTransformation` + source/target lines; `PurchaseRequestRevision`; `PurchaseOrderAmendment` + amendment lines; `ContractSpecificationRevision`; `ContractObligationEntry`; `QuotationEvaluationSnapshot`; `ServiceAcceptanceActLine`; `SupplierClaimResolution` + resolution lines. Extend existing `QualityClaim` with typed claim reason and claim lines; use the same resolution service for `ServiceClaim` rather than copying it. All terminal execution facts link a source posting/acceptance/adjustment ID and reversal-of ID.

### 3.2 MM-03-01: supplier admission and performance

Keep supplier profile, multiple bank details, tax/branch duplicate handling, evidence uploads, compliance checks, accreditation scopes/expiry, activation/block/unblock and all approval paths. Enforce contextual new-business eligibility at invitation, award, contract activation/release and PO dispatch/amendment; preserve separate existing-obligation eligibility at receipt/return/claim.

Add a versioned performance projection from delivery-schedule allocations, accepted/rejected inspection results and their reversals. Present explicit numerator/denominator/date basis: quantity delivered on/before promised date divided by due quantity; finally accepted quantity divided by finally inspected quantity; quantity-weighted days late; open/resolved claim count and age. Goods use document/base units within a material/UOM cohort; do not add kilograms to pieces. Overall supplier timely-delivery percentage is value-weighted using frozen PO base-currency values when heterogeneous units are combined, with weighting disclosed. Undue tranches and uninspected quantities are excluded and shown separately. Service/works measures use accepted milestones and their promised dates.

Keep legacy composite score with its formula version visible. New KPIs do not silently change supplier admission thresholds; changing that policy requires a reviewed version. Receipt/quality/return reversal triggers recalculation without duplicate rating events. Do not assign arbitrary adverse scores just for sending a claim; show substantiated outcome and pending allegation distinctly.

### 3.3 MM-03-02: merge, split, revision and remaining cancellation

`POST /api/purchase-requests/transformations/preview` accepts mode MERGE/SPLIT, source line IDs, requested quantities, target departments/dates and versions; returns conservation, budget and approval impact. `POST /api/purchase-requests/transformations` persists a transformation draft; `/{id}/submit` uses the approval engine; approval applies it atomically. These are **proposed routes**.

Merge compatible requests into one header with separate origin allocations; split one request into multiple target headers/lines. Preserve original line/approval history and consume only the available source slice. Do not clone a source's full entitlement into each child. Compatibility key includes company, request/business type, material/service specification, currency, UOM snapshot, destination/earmark and demand-cycle rules. Multiple departments may share a procurement package but retain separate allocations, department visibility and approvals. Original requests remain navigable as partially transferred/superseded evidence.

Approved commercial changes use `POST /api/purchase-requests/{id}/revisions`, then submission and approval, retaining old authorization until the revision applies. Quantity increases require section 02's newly approved demand coverage. Reductions/cancellations affect only unallocated quantity; allocated slices require downstream release/cancellation first. Cancellation of the remaining portion must be possible without cancelling fulfilled history. Approval moves/resizes budget reservation once, and rejection releases any provisional hold. Existing direct sourcing bypasses remain rejected.

### 3.4 MM-03-03: evaluation and award

Persist an evaluation snapshot per RFQ revision: accepted current quotation revision IDs, line coverage, normalized quantity/UOM, original net/gross price/tax/currency, comparison currency/rate/date/source, delivery/freight/incoterm assumptions, payment/warranty terms, disqualification reasons and approved selection rule/weights. Unsupported conversion or missing commercial information gives a blocking reason, never a zero price or best rank.

`POST /api/rfq/{id}/evaluation-snapshots` creates a draft comparison; submit/approval uses tender governance where required. Existing selection and contract-award routes consume an approved snapshot and expected RFQ/quote versions. Lowest-price uses comparable landed commercial cost with disclosed inclusions, not incomparable raw totals. BEST_SCORE keeps its governed scoring criteria; a non-lowest award requires the appropriate rule and recorded reason, not an ad hoc bypass. Split awards use explicit line quantities and existing lineage arithmetic, conserving eligible RFQ source quantity. Re-publication/amendment invalidates affected evaluation readiness and requires re-evaluation without deleting sealed-opening history.

### 3.5 MM-03-04: contractual commitment and fulfillment

An activated contract/specification is an immutable commercial revision. Addenda propose effective-dated changes to validity, total amount, specification quantities/prices/service stages and terms, use separate approval, then apply once on/after the effective date. Conflicting overlapping revision windows are rejected. Reductions below irreversible fulfillment or live commitments are blocked with linked documents. Backdating never silently reprices posted receipts/acts; section 09 owns explicit value corrections.

`ContractObligationEntry` records amount and quantity at specification level: RESERVE, RELEASE, FULFILL, FULFILL_REVERSAL, and commercial ADJUSTMENT with source/reversal reference. Reserve when a PO/release is approved for dispatch (or when a framework call-off is approved); fulfillment consumes the reservation into executed quantity/amount, not a second use of the ceiling. Exposure = outstanding committed + executed not reversed. Availability = approved ceiling − exposure. Cancellation releases only the unfulfilled reservation. A return for replacement moves fulfillment back to outstanding commitment; a return for credit also needs approved termination of that obligation before releasing capacity. Financial credit alone does not authorize another purchase.

Preserve framework ledger/history but document existing CONSUME as released/called-off commercial capacity, not received quantity. Expose new fulfillment separately; migrate projections without double counting old COMMIT + CONSUME. Legacy entries require reconciliation by PO and source fact, not a blanket reset. Framework reversal needs PO cancellation/amendment evidence and must not restore fulfilled capacity merely by reversing a call-off record.

`POST /api/contracts/{id}/releases/preview` and `/releases` accept selected specification revisions, quantities, delivery dates and idempotency key, supporting several bounded POs for an ordinary contract. Extend framework call-offs to accept the same line release payload and preserve single/ranked multi-supplier policy. Keep existing create-purchase-order compatibility behavior until its callers migrate, but route it through the same capacity guard.

Close/suspend commands show open PO quantities, claims, pending amendments, unused commitments and invoice discrepancies. Suspension blocks new commitments but preserves controlled execution of existing obligations. Closing requires all commercial obligations settled or explicitly short-closed; unpaid but undisputed finance obligations can remain visible as finance-owned records.

### 3.6 MM-03-05: sent-order amendments, schedules and completion

Add `POST /api/purchase-orders/{id}/amendments` (base commercial version, lines, reason); `.../amendments/{amendmentId}/submit`; approval creates a pending dispatch snapshot. Supplier confirmation and buyer acceptance make that commercial revision effective; previous agreed terms remain effective while negotiation is pending. Draft changes are editable; approved/dispatched evidence is immutable. A supplier rejection leaves the prior revision in force. Increases reserve provisional contract/budget capacity while approval/confirmation is outstanding; abandon/reject releases that hold once.

Stable PO lines cannot change supplier, currency or material/service identity after execution; create a replacement order for those changes. Remaining quantity, future schedules and future price slices may change with approval. No reduction below already received/accepted quantity or irrevocable in-flight commitments; first resolve in-flight ASN/commitment cancellation or return. Price changes apply only to unfulfilled allocations, with effective revision evidence. Receipt posting and amendment activation lock the same PO/line guard and revalidate quantities/version.

Keep `SupplierPoDispatchService`, response/buyer decision/supersession services and commitment-linked schedules. One commercial revision can have several deliveries; all active confirmed tranches must fit its remaining quantity. Preserve partially fulfilled old commitments and ASN links; supersede only their unfulfilled remainder. A stale supplier response cannot overwrite the effective revision.

Add `POST /api/purchase-orders/{id}/short-close` with explicit line remainder, reason, expected version and approval reference. It releases residual commitments/budget/contract capacity and updates section 02 uncovered demand; it never manufactures receipt. A fully received order may close operationally while invoicing/payment remain independently open. Existing closeout procedures retain their stronger business/finance requirements. No full cancellation of a partially executed order; use cancellation of its remaining obligation plus retained postings.

### 3.7 MM-03-06: actual service/work evidence

Keep quantity-, amount- and milestone-based service intake/execution/partial acceptance and works acts. Add `ServiceAcceptanceActLine` linking accepted execution/milestone, PO line, contract specification revision, performed period, accepted/rejected quantity or amount, UOM, price, tax and evidence. Approve technical acceptance separately from document/finance review. Accepted act lines become section 09 invoice evidence; finance status and paid coverage remain separate projections.

At approval lock the PO/specification and relevant acceptance origin so two acts cannot consume the same remaining volume. An act cannot allocate more than its accepted execution/milestone evidence, PO remaining amount/quantity or contractual stage limit. Rejected work is not invoice-eligible. Rework references rejected evidence and subsequent acceptance, avoiding double count. For fixed-price amount milestones, no artificial stock quantity is required.

Use `POST /api/service-acts/{id}/corrections` and corresponding work-act correction command for approved evidence. A correction creates negative allocations referencing original lines and, if necessary, a new positive act. It preserves document/finance approvals and period restrictions. Draft rollback remains for unposted/unapproved editing; financial markers cannot be cleared to consume twice. A paid/invoiced act correction creates a finance review/credit obligation through section 09; it does not execute payment reversal. No service/works acceptance creates material stock unless a separate section 04/07 material receipt explicitly exists.

### 3.8 MM-03-07: evidenced resolution of supplier claims

Extend quality claims with reason SHORT_DELIVERY/QUALITY/COMMERCIAL_TERMS and lines referencing PO/schedule, receipt/inspection/act, affected quantity, claimed amount/currency and due date. A shortage can reference a confirmed obligation without a stock movement; a defect return must reference physically received units. Service claims use the same resolution allocation contract with service/works origin.

`POST /api/quality/claims/{id}/resolution-plans` records quantity/amount lines and resolution type: replacement, supplier return plus credit, price adjustment, rework, or documented rejection/waiver. Submit and approve the plan before execution. `GET .../{id}/resolution-evidence` reports required/received evidence, remaining quantities and financial status. Service-claim routes delegate to the same command service.

Section 08 posts authorized returns; section 04 records replacement receipt; section 09 posts invoice/value adjustments. Section 03 consumes their immutable evidence and never changes stock or payment state directly. Split resolution is allowed, for example 4 of 10 units replaced and 6 returned for credit. A plan line can require both physical and financial evidence; these do not count as two independently resolved quantities. Each affected unit/amount has one final disposition, unless an explicit reversal reopens it. Replacement is linked to the original outstanding obligation with commercial price treatment recorded, not an unrelated free PO.

Claim state: DRAFT → SENT → ACKNOWLEDGED/UNDER_RESOLUTION → RESOLVED → CLOSED, with explicit rejection/dispute/CAPA branches. Retain existing external status compatibility while exposing a separate resolution status. ACCEPTED means supplier accepted liability, not that stock/finance settlement occurred. CAPA may be open while physical resolution progresses; closure checks both required CAPA and disposition evidence. Supplier rejection needs authorized buyer acceptance/waiver with reason to close; unresolved disputes remain open. Delete only unsubmitted drafts. Reversal of a linked return/replacement/credit reopens the appropriate unresolved slice and audit trail.

## 4. Default policy choices for the combined user review

These are proposed defaults, not already approved enterprise policy. They preserve every requested process and identify decisions the coordinator must present together.

| Topic | Proposed default and consequence |
|---|---|
| Manual purchase demand | Keep demand-backed approval as the normal path. Emergency/ad hoc demand is an explicit expedited section 02 approval origin with reason, owner and budget; never a hidden bypass. |
| PR merge/split | Only free source quantity can move; transformed targets require approval, preserving original demand attribution. Cross-company merge is prohibited; cross-department packages retain department lines and access. |
| New supplier business | Require active applicable accreditation/KYC/readiness at each new commitment. Expired accreditation on an existing obligation produces the existing contextual policy result. Hard blocks/archival are not silently bypassed; warehouse containment/return exceptions require recorded authorized disposition agreed with section 08. |
| Award comparison | Freeze rate/date/source and use approved comparison currency, net tax treatment and stated freight assumptions. Missing FX/UOM/tax basis blocks automatic ranking. Single-source and ranked frameworks remain governed methods. |
| Limits | Zero is not a new contract's implicit unlimited ceiling. Unlimited requires an explicit authorized flag/reason; legacy zero-limit contracts enter review and remain clearly identified. Goods, services and works reserve future obligations consistently. |
| PO amendments | Material increases, price changes and reductions all require reapproval; dispatched commercial changes require supplier reconfirmation. No retroactive changes to accepted evidence. |
| Delivery tolerance | Zero overdelivery and zero automatic short-close tolerance by default. Authorized tolerance profiles may be added as explicit section 04/09 policy; do not silently accept rounding overrun. |
| Due-date KPI | Use confirmed schedule date, falling back to approved PO date with a visible fallback flag; never move historical due dates to erase lateness. Supplier-attributable exclusions need approved reason/evidence. |
| Service completion | Operational completion is accepted quantity/milestones; document review, invoice eligibility, invoicing and payment are separately visible. Preserve external finance statuses without making paid amount the fulfillment source. |
| Claim resolution | Require posted evidence for each chosen remedy and required CAPA completion before closure. No automatic waiver, stock return or financial credit from a status change. |
| Reversal/period | Corrections carry original references and current authorized operation date; closed periods follow section 09 reopening/correction policy. No backdated silent edits. |
| Notifications/providers | Reuse outbox, authenticated callbacks and controlled retries. Provider delivery confirmation is distinct from local queuing; live email/signature/treasury acceptance remains an explicit verification item. |

## 5. Permissions, audit and concurrency

Use existing module/action roles plus scoped domain guards; section 10 owns the common matrix and approval adapter conventions. `B/security/SecurityConfig.java:239` separates PO receiving roles; `:257`–`:263` separates procurement and supplier scopes; `B/common/BusinessEntity.java:54` provides optimistic versioning. These baselines do not alone prove all new endpoints are safe.

| Actor | Permitted responsibility |
|---|---|
| Department requester/head | Create/view own demand-backed requests, propose changes, see attributed fulfillment; cannot award or self-approve. |
| Procurement specialist/manager | Prepare transformations, comparisons, PO changes, contract releases and claims within company/department data scope. Approval only through an assigned independent task. |
| Supplier/compliance manager | Supplier identity/evidence, admission/block decisions according to existing workflow; no unilateral warehouse or finance posting. |
| Technical/service commission | Accept/reject actual service/work evidence within assignment and department scope; cannot execute treasury settlement. |
| Warehouse staff | See commercial references needed to receive/return within allowed warehouses; cannot amend commercial quantity/price or award. |
| Finance/budget controller | Approve budget/financial exception/credit evidence under sections 09–10; payment execution remains external finance ownership. |
| Supplier portal user | Read their own published dispatch/RFQ and submit responses/ASN/evidence; never internal comparisons, other suppliers' bids, internal budget or unrestricted contracts. |
| Auditor/viewer | Read permitted history/evidence, no transitions. Admin does not bypass immutable postings or separation of duty. |

Every mutation rechecks tenant/current company and parent-child matching; external IDs must not widen company scope. Warehouse/destination and source department access are checked at the command boundary. Foreign scoped objects return the established non-disclosing not-found response. Stale version returns 409 with current revision/reload advice; state/capacity failure returns the standard localized business error and blocker references. The frontend preserves draft input on conflict and requires review of the changed server state.

Require command idempotency key + normalized payload hash, scoped by tenant/company/command/aggregate. Same key/same payload returns the original result; same key/different payload conflicts. Approvals, postings, budget entries and outbox records are atomic with the business mutation. Store before/after commercial snapshot, actor, delegated authority/task, reason, versions, operation/registration times and correlation ID. Generic PUT/status/delete routes must not circumvent command guards.

All cross-line transformations lock parent/source entitlement before allocating in deterministic order. PO amendment activation, receiving, return-derived fulfillment reversal and service acceptance share the same order-line capacity guard. Contract exposure reserves/fulfills/releases under parent/specification locks; framework and ordinary-contract projections cannot reserve the same source twice. Unique constraints cover active amendment per base revision, command key, source posting + evidence kind + line, and reversal reference/allocation identity. Partial reversals carry cumulative bounds under lock; a unique reversal key alone is not enough. Lock ordering and shared port names must be reconciled with sections 04/05/09/10 before coding.
